# Icon brief: Orbit

Product: A source-native office where a person and AI work together · Audience: young knowledge workers · Category neighbors: generic AI sparkles, chat bubbles, document sheets, atom icons

## Concepts

### 1. Orbital link (axis: none)

Depicts: interlocking orbits

Metaphor: Two independent peers move through parallel paths that cross around one shared source.

Gestalt device: continuity

16px risk: The inner counters may close if the filled orbital bands become too heavy.

Distinct from: It avoids AI sparkles, chat bubbles, initials, document corners, and radial atom spokes through one diagonal linked silhouette.

Not a UI glyph: Checked against link, refresh, share, sync, and infinity controls; the paired planetary nodes keep it product-specific.

### 2. Travelling peers (axis: none)

Depicts: binary orbit

Metaphor: Two planetary bodies sit on separate intersecting tracks rather than inside them, emphasizing motion and equal agency.

Gestalt device: continuity

16px risk: The two planetary nodes must remain distinguishable from the orbital bands.

Distinct from: Unlike a conventional atom, both paths share one angle and never radiate from a nucleus.

Not a UI glyph: Checked against link, refresh, share, sync, and infinity controls; the offset bodies break those conventional silhouettes.

### 3. Orbital knot (axis: none)

Depicts: orbital knot

Metaphor: Two paths become one continuous binary orbit, with a planet carried on each turn.

Gestalt device: continuity

16px risk: The inner loop counters must remain open rather than collapsing into one solid bow.

Distinct from: It replaces atom spokes and generic overlapping ellipses with one nameable woven silhouette.

Not a UI glyph: Checked against link, refresh, share, sync, and infinity controls; the unequal node positions and orbital counters prevent a plain infinity reading.

### 4. Orbital knot with planetary nodes (axis: none)

Depicts: orbital knot

Metaphor: One continuous shared motion contains two distinct planetary actors, marked without changing the silhouette.

Gestalt device: continuity

16px risk: The restrained coral nodes may reduce to single pixels but the primary knot must remain intact.

Distinct from: A dark continuous-corner field and two embedded nodes distinguish it from a generic infinity control.

Not a UI glyph: Checked against link, refresh, share, sync, and infinity controls; the asymmetric node positions turn the loop into a product mark.

### 5. Parallel orbits (axis: none)

Depicts: parallel orbits

Metaphor: Two independent tracks follow one inclination, cross twice, and each visibly carries one peer.

Gestalt device: continuity

16px risk: The crossings must remain open and the nodes must not merge into the bands.

Distinct from: It uses two equal parallel ellipses rather than the radial spokes and central nucleus of a generic atom.

Not a UI glyph: Checked against link, refresh, share, sync, and infinity controls; the two coral planetary nodes make its purpose specific.

## Recommendation

Compare Orbital knot with planetary nodes against Parallel orbits at 16px. The knot has the stronger silhouette; Parallel orbits follows the requested metaphor more literally and should win if its crossings remain clean.
